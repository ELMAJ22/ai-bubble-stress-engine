      Limitations (please read)
      • Anchors and weights are the author's hypotheses. The index has not been backtested.
      • Only about half of the planned indicators are live. Utilization, token economics, productivity and venture flows are not measured yet.
      • Many indicators are analyst judgments, and lab revenue is run rate, not recognized revenue.
      • Low confidence means the number should be read as a range. An earlier robustness test of 1,000 random weightings gave a 5th to 95th percentile of 44 to 68 around a median of 57.
      • Conflict of interest: the analysis was built with Claude (Anthropic), and Anthropic's IPO is the subject of one indicator.
      • Not investment advice.
      Data and method: github.com/ELMAJ22/ai-bubble-stress-engine. Updated automatically each weekday by a free GitHub Actions job. Shiller CAPE and credit spreads are automatic; other indicators are refreshed by hand.
